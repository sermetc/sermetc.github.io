privacy policy

Last updated: March 10, 2026

Sermet Cagan ("we", "us", or "our") operates the papers mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our app.

1. data collection

papers is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect or store any of your personal data on our servers. All information you provide within the app is stored locally on your device or in your private iCloud storage if enabled.

  • Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, or phone numbers.
  • Usage Data: We do not track your browsing or reading habits on our servers.
  • Papers Data: The app fetches data directly from external academic databases. Your search queries and reading history are private to your device.

2. icloud sync

If you choose to use iCloud synchronization, your bookmarks and preferences are stored in your personal iCloud account. We do not have access to this data.

3. third-party services

The app interacts with the following third-party services:

  • External API: To fetch academic papers. Please refer to the provider's privacy policy for their data practices.
  • Apple App Store: For app distribution and updates.

4. notifications

The app uses local and push notifications to alert you of new papers. These are managed through Apple's Push Notification service. We do not use these to send marketing material.

5. children's privacy

Our app does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

6. changes to this privacy policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
