“Capture moments, preserve memories.”

Lume is built around a simple idea: journaling should feel as immediate as taking a photo, and as tactile as holding onto something real. The app borrows the warmth of a polaroid, the speed of a camera, and the intimacy of a handwritten note.

Why it feels different

Not another grid of disposable photos.

01

Shoot first

Start with the camera. Each entry begins as a photo, then becomes a memory card instead of a feed post.

02

Shake to develop

The onboarding and capture flow lean into the old instant-film ritual, turning loading into a little bit of theater.

03

Flip and write

Double tap, turn the card over, and leave a typed or handwritten-feeling note on the back.

04

Add a voice

Record up to 30 seconds of audio and keep the tone of the moment, not just the text that describes it.

05

Browse by time

Move through memories in day, month, grouped, or all-entry views, then filter by favorites, audio, or tags.

06

Return every day

Daily reminders and streaks nudge the habit without making the app feel like homework.

The product rhythm

A fast ritual from capture to archive.

1. Capture

Open straight into a camera-first experience framed like a vintage instant camera.

2. Develop

Watch the shot settle into a soft, polaroid-like card with a small moment of motion.

3. Annotate

Flip the memory, write a note, attach tags, or record a short voice memo.

4. Collect

Sort the growing stack by date, favorites, audio notes, or tags and export what matters.

Built for quiet documentation

Lume makes everyday memory-keeping feel analog again.

Designed around polaroid cards, handwritten notes, soft gradients, and small rituals, it gives ordinary moments enough weight to stick.
